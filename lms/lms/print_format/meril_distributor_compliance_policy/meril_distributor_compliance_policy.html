MERIL DISTRIBUTOR COMPLIANCE POLICY

Healthcare professionals (HCP) and Healthcare Organizations (HCO) can be engaged for the below scientific activities

1.0 Sponsorship to 3rd party congress/conference/Meetings:

Sponsorship supports to 3rd party congress/conferences/Meetings are intended to share and spread advancement and technology among medical fraternity. It also gives the Sponsor an opportunity to showcase its products and advancement in technology. Following are important points to be kept in mind while extending Sponsorship support:

  • Sponsorship support to be provided only for scientific and educational program/ conference/ congresses.
  • The scientific content/topics at a conference should be aligned to the products or therapies in which Meril is dealing.
  • Sponsorship amount should be modest and at par with the offerings to other sponsors.
  • In lieu of the sponsorship amount, entitlement such as Stall, Training village, Scientific Session should be provided.
  • Proper accounting along with required supporting documents should be maintained for record.
  • The following points shall be kept in mind while providing sponsorship support
    • ❖ Sponsorship amount can only be provided to Health Care Organization (HCO).
    • ❖ Sponsorship amount shall not be given as a reward or inducement for usage of Meril's products or services ;
    • ❖ Sponsorship amount shall not be given for the purpose of Off-label Promotion of a Product;
    • ❖ Sponsorship amount shall not be given in exchange of influencing regulatory, pricing, formulary or reimbursement decisions
2.0 Training and Education:

Training and education programs are Meril initiated programs, focused in the safe and effective use of Meril's products. Such Training and Education Programs can include hands on sessions, lectures and presentations on topics related to Meril's products or therapies. Following are important points to be kept in mind while extending support for Training and Educational programs:

  • Content/topics of Training and Education (T & E) program should be aligned to Meril's products or therapies.
  • Expenses for such T&E program should be recorded in books with necessary supporting documents.
  • Venue should be conducive to conduct training and educational programs,
  • Reasonable travel and modest lodging can be arranged for attending HCPs.

  • Training and education programs should not be organized to secure an improper advantage or to please HCPs.
3.0 Grant (Monetary/Non-Monetary):

Grant (Monetary/Non-Monetary) can be provided to Health Care Organizations (hereinafter "HCO") including and not limited to, a hospital, university, medical associations, institute or organization) for Scientific, Educational, Charity and better patient outcome purpose.

  • Grant should be provided for the purpose of supporting Scientific, Educational and Charitable activities which in turn brings better healthcare outcome and benefits to the patients.
  • Grant support has to be properly documented and recorded in books with necessary supporting documents.
  • The following points shall be kept in mind while providing Grant
    • • Grant can only be provided to Healthcare Organization (HCO)
    • • Grant amount shall not be given as a reward or inducement for usage of Meril's products or services ;
    • • Grant amount shall not be given for the purpose of Off-label Promotion of a Product;
    • • Grant amount shall not be given for influencing regulatory, pricing, formulary or reimbursement decisions.
4.0 Awareness Program:

Therapy Awareness programs are designed to increase awareness amongst general public with respect to underlying clinical conditions and innovation in medical field with treatment options and therapies.

  • Therapy Awareness Programs should be conducted in collaboration with HCP experienced in relevant therapy.
  • Awareness Programs can be designed in the form of Awareness Camps for Patients, Article Publications in newspapers and Magazines, Radio Activity.
  • Awareness program should not lead to any publicity/promotion/profiling to any HCP or HCO. Ensure to follow THE NATIONAL MEDICAL COMMISSION ACT, 2019. Only HCP name, Designation, Associated Hospital name and small size photo can be included where awareness article contents are provided by HCP In case of Article Publications in Newspapers and Magazines.
  • Product promotion to general public is not allowed subject to provision of The DRUGS AND MAGIC REMEDIES ACT, 1954.
  • The DRUGS AND MAGIC REMEDIES ACT, 1954 prohibit any false or misleading advertisement.
  • Expenses for such programs should be recorded in books with required supporting documents
5.0 HCP Services:

Engaging HCPs to provide valuable bona fide services such as a speaker, proctor, trainer, advisor, etc. During scientific, training or education program on effective use of Meril products and therapy. Following points to be kept in mind while considering HCP's engagement:

  • Identify and document legitimate business need and purpose for HCP service.
  • Selection of HCP should be on the basis of the HCPs qualifications and expertise in relevant field to address the identified purpose and should not be on the basis of past or potential purchases.
  • HCP agreement must be in writing, clearly defining scope of work and services to be performed and compensation to be provided. HCP service agreement has to be signed off by all the parties prior to the commencement of services post internal approval.
  • Compensation/service fees paid to HCPs must be consistent with the fair market value.
  • HCP service fees/compensation shall not be paid without valid agreement and HCP work completion report.

For all the above mentioned activities kindly reach out to Meril Business Manager.
